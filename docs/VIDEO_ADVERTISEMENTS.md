## Production Specifications

### Video Format
- **Resolution**: 4K (3840x2160) for main videos, 1080p for social
- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - 16:9 (YouTube, website)
  - 9:16 (Instagram Stories, TikTok)
  - 1:1 (Instagram Feed, LinkedIn)
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps (web)
- **Codec**: H.264 (MP4)

### Audio
- **Music**: Licensed from Artlist, Epidemic Sound, or AudioJungle
- **Voiceover**: Professional VO artist (Voices.com, Fiverr Pro)
- **Mix**: Voiceover -3dB, Music -18dB, SFX -12dB

### Graphics
- **Brand Colors**:
  - Primary: #092c47 (dark blue)
  - Accent: #13bf87 (green)
  - Secondary: #ffc118 (yellow)
- **Fonts**:
  - Headings: Helvetica Bold
  - Body: Inter Regular
- **Logo**: Always use official Loombotic logo with clearspace

### Accessibility
- **Captions**: Burned-in captions for all videos (83% watch on mute)
- **Audio Description**: Available for key marketing videos
- **Contrast**: WCAG AA compliant text overlays

## Distribution Strategy

### Paid Advertising
- **YouTube Ads**: Pre-roll on engineering/tech channels
- **LinkedIn Ads**: Sponsored content targeting engineers, procurement
- **Google Display Network**: Remarketing to website visitors
- **Trade Shows**: Loop videos on booth displays

### Organic
- **YouTube Channel**: Full library of tutorial and case study videos
- **LinkedIn**: Short clips with engineering insights
- **Website**: Embedded on homepage, product pages, about page
- **Email**: Embedded in onboarding sequences

### Retargeting
- Show case study videos to users who got quotes but didn't order
- Show tutorial videos to users who abandoned during upload
- Show brand video to all website visitors in general campaigns

---

## Performance Metrics

### Key KPIs
- **View Rate**: >30% (YouTube pre-roll)
- **Completion Rate**: >75% (organic)
- **Click-Through Rate**: >2% (ads)
- **Cost Per Click**: <$2.50
- **Cost Per Acquisition**: <$150

### A/B Testing
- Test different hooks in first 5 seconds
- Test male vs female voiceover
- Test technical vs emotional messaging
- Test video length (30s vs 60s vs 90s)
